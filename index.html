About

Plasma physics across propulsion, nonlinear dynamics, and computation.

I am a theoretical and computational plasma physicist currently working at the Asia Pacific Center for Theoretical Physics (APCTP), Pohang. My present research focuses on magnetohydrodynamic modelling of a magnetic reconnection thruster.

I completed my Ph.D. in Plasma Physics at IIT Delhi, where I studied flowing magnetized plasmas and finite-size plasma dynamics. My research also includes electric propulsion, magnetic nozzles, classical trajectory Monte Carlo modelling, computational fluid dynamics, and quantum graph and hypergraph states.
